Constraining the dynamics of galaxy cluster mergers

ICM-SHOX — Improved Constraints on Mergers with SZ, Hydrodynamical simulations, Optical, and X-ray — is a systematic framework for reconstructing the 3D geometry, epoch, and velocities of merging galaxy clusters. Cluster mergers are the most energetic events since the Big Bang and act as natural laboratories for studying dark matter, plasma physics, and cosmology — but any single observation only tells part of the story. By jointly comparing X-ray, thermal and kinetic Sunyaev–Zel'dovich (SZ), optical, gravitational lensing, and radio observations against tailored hydrodynamical simulations, ICM-SHOX constrains merger parameters that no single probe can constrain on its own.
